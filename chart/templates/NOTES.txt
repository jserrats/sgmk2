  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "sgmk2-cv.fullname" . }} 5000:{{ .Values.service.port }}

This chart always runs a single replica with the Recreate strategy: the app uses a fixed
MQTT client_id, so two pods would keep disconnecting each other from the broker. Expect a
short gap in control during upgrades.

The camera ({{ .Values.camera.url }}) must be reachable from inside the pod. If its
name doesn't resolve, set hostAliases, dnsPolicy/dnsConfig, or hostNetwork in your values.
